## Changes in this pass

- **21 MCQs added:** the 20 distinct questions on scanned 32/6/1 pages
  5–19, and 32/5/1 Q5 on page 5. The same 20 questions recur on 32/6/2
  and 32/6/3 in different question order; they are not added again. A
  same-stem question from a different year can have different choices and is
  not evidence for the answer position. These 21 have empty answers, as no
  qualifying marking scheme was supplied.
- **7 existing MCQs visually compared in full** against 32/5/1 pages 3 and 5:
  Q1–4 and Q6–8. Six needed English stem/option repairs; Q4 already matched.
  All seven and the 21 additions carry a text-verification marker only. The
  checked questions and source page numbers are in the 3251 and 3261 fixtures.
- **55 existing rows corrected from 2025 to 2023:** 49 text-layer matches at
  the same paper, set, question number, and normalized stem/options (22 from
  32/2, 20 from 32/4, seven from 32/5); plus the six visually repaired 32/5/1
  rows above. Q4 overlaps the 49 matches and is counted once. The 49
  comparisons and PDF page numbers are in `social-2023-year-matches.json`.
  This comparison supports provenance, not a promise that extraction captured
  every glyph. These 49 were not generally marked source-verified. Rows with
  incomplete tables, pictures or ambiguous extraction were excluded.
- **0 board keys attached or verified**. The fixtures are regression records,
  not independent evidence of correctness; the original PDFs remain the source.

This is a **partial audit**, not a claim that all 15 papers have been fully
transcribed or reconciled: 32/1, most of 32/2, 32/4 and 32/5, and other years
and mixed-year rows still need careful review. In particular, 2025 labels on
other rows should not be changed wholesale based on paper code. Scanned 32/6
sets 2 and 3 were inspected for ordering, but not separately represented in
the bank. Figures and table-dependent rows need original context. The previous
verification reports describe earlier upload batches and their separate counts.
