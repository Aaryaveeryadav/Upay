# Online CBSE marking-scheme search and 2022 Social Science uploads

Search date: 17 November 2026. [CBSE's examination marking-scheme index](https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/marking-scheme.html) lists Class X and XII subject archives for main and supplementary exams from 2022 onward. A listing establishes that a source exists; it does **not** by itself verify any bank answer or guarantee that all sets are present. The archives are ZIP files and their contents were not independently opened in this environment. Sample-paper marking schemes are different documents, not substitutes for main-board keys.

## 28 Social keys admitted from printed marking-scheme copies

The previously supplied 2023 32/6/1 question paper was transcribed and reviewed in the preceding pass. Its [printed marking scheme](https://cdn.savemyexams.com/uploads/2026/01/52805-32_6_1%20SOCIAL%20SCIENCE%20Feb%202023%20CBSE%20class%2010%20MS.pdf) (third printed page) lists Q1–20. Separately, the [32/5/1 marking scheme](https://cdn.savemyexams.com/uploads/2026/01/6556-32_5_1%20SOCIAL%20SCIENCE%20Feb%202023%20CBSE%20class%2010%20MS.pdf) lists Q1–8, previously compared in full with the uploaded paper. These are third-party hosted copies of printed schemes, not downloaded from CBSE's ZIP archive. The [mirror's index](https://www.savemyexams.com/class-10/social-studies/cbse/social-science/past-papers/) labels their year and set; the 2023 bank's full options and correct-position text match the printed entries. Q11's source paraphrases the correct option but has the same meaning and letter.

Added **28 verified key fields** to those exact 28 already-present records: 20 for 32/6/1, 8 for 32/5/1. No new questions added in this pass. `tools/check/fixtures/social-2023-board-keys.json` records paper, question, printed letter, answer text and page; `social_2023_verification.js` tests the text, option order, key, provenance and quality gate. No key was inferred using AI. No key is copied to another year's reused code. These records can use the existing local verified-key grading path when eligible; they are not a promise that every other subject or set is keyed. Remaining archive files still need question-by-question review.

## Twelve new uploads

The newly uploaded files are **question papers, not marking schemes**: 13 questions, 40 marks, 2 hours, with short/long, case and map questions rather than the full-exam MCQ section. Covers show AQQPP/1, CQQPP/2, BQQPP/3 and DQQPP/4 respectively. The [2022 paper index](https://pyqpapers.com/cbse-class-10-previous-year-question-papers-2022/) and [contemporaneous term-II description](https://www.collegedekho.com/news/cbse-10th-social-science-term-2-exam-on-may-14-download-sample-paper-marking-scheme-26447/) corroborate 2022 term II; file creation dates are corroborating metadata, not year proof alone. Page 2 of each set-1 file was also checked. The mirror's index calls them February 2022, but these are the May 2022 term-II papers; do not reuse the mirror's month label as provenance.

We examined the [32/1/1](https://cdn.savemyexams.com/uploads/2026/01/15962-32_1_1_Social%20Science%20Feb%202022%20CBSE%20Class%2010%20MS.pdf) and [32/2/1](https://cdn.savemyexams.com/uploads/2026/01/5413-32-2-1_Social%20Science%20Feb%202022%20CBSE%20Class%2010%20MS.pdf) printed scheme copies; paper and first questions match the uploads, but these are descriptive marking points and were **not** turned into MCQ keys. The mirror index lists other set copies; those were not individually verified. Existing 2022-looking records incorrectly labelled 2025 remain to be reviewed individually.
