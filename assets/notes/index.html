Solutions

Solutions are classified by their components and described using concentration measures. Solubility, vapour pressure and ideal behaviour lead to colligative properties, which help determine molar mass; association or dissociation can cause abnormal results.

Sections

  • Types of solutions
  • Expression of concentration
  • Solubility
  • Vapour pressure of liquid solutions
  • Ideal and non-ideal solutions
  • Colligative properties and molar mass
  • Abnormal molecular masses

Objectives

  • Express solution concentration in different units.
  • Explain Henry’s law, Raoult’s law and deviations from ideal behaviour.
  • Relate colligative properties to molar mass and abnormal molecular mass.

Key terms

Solute · Solvent · Mole fraction · Molality · Henry’s law · Raoult’s law · Colligative properties · van’t Hoff factor

Electrochemistry

Electrochemical cells connect chemical reactions with electrical energy. Cell potentials and the Nernst equation describe galvanic cells; conductance, electrolysis, batteries, fuel cells and corrosion connect electrical measurements with chemical change.

Sections

  • Electrochemical cells
  • Galvanic cells
  • Nernst equation
  • Conductance of electrolytic solutions
  • Electrolytic cells and electrolysis
  • Batteries
  • Fuel cells
  • Corrosion

Objectives

  • Distinguish galvanic and electrolytic cells.
  • Apply the Nernst equation and relate cell potential to Gibbs energy.
  • Explain conductance, electrolysis, batteries, fuel cells and corrosion.

Key terms

Galvanic cell · Electrode potential · Nernst equation · Conductivity · Molar conductivity · Kohlrausch law · Electrolysis · Fuel cell · Corrosion

Chemical kinetics examines reaction rates and the factors that affect them. Rate laws, order and molecularity describe reactions; integrated equations for zero and first order and the Arrhenius relationship help explain how concentration and temperature influence rates.

Sections

  • Rate of a chemical reaction
  • Factors influencing reaction rate
  • Integrated rate equations
  • Temperature dependence of reaction rate
  • Collision theory

Objectives

  • Distinguish average and instantaneous reaction rates.
  • Explain rate laws, order and molecularity and factors affecting rate.
  • Use zero- and first-order integrated equations and describe temperature dependence and collision theory.

Key terms

Reaction rate · Rate law · Order · Molecularity · Rate constant · Half-life · Activation energy · Arrhenius equation · Collision theory
